# Live judging prompts

Use these one-shot briefs to verify that the dashboard chooses a different perspective for each role. All data is illustrative.

1. **Sales manager:** Build a weekly dashboard for Dana, our West-region sales manager. Show bookings against target, pipeline coverage, rep performance, and the deals that need attention before Friday.
2. **Marketing director:** I am deciding where to spend next month's marketing budget. Compare paid search, social, email, events, and organic by spend, qualified leads, CAC, and conversion rate. Identify the campaigns to scale or pause.
3. **Support lead:** Create a morning dashboard for Mina, customer support lead. Prioritize urgent open tickets and SLA breaches, show response time trends, agent workloads, and a table of tickets to escalate today.
4. **Executive / mixed:** Give our COO an executive pulse covering sales bookings, marketing efficiency, and support backlog. Show where customer experience is putting revenue at risk and the three decisions to make this week.
5. **Specific constraint:** I manage enterprise renewals. Show six accounts with renewal dates, contract value, health, and owner; a renewal forecast; and the top three at-risk accounts. Make the account table the main focus.

Check that titles match the role, requested priorities appear, trends and totals agree, and the entire dashboard renders at desktop and phone sizes. A fallback must be disclosed. Invalid or failed requests must leave a usable screen and allow retry.
